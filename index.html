Interpretation & limitations

Weight sizing and attention-cache arithmetic follow published equations. Activation, runtime, quantization metadata, LoRA parameter count, sharding communication, and performance efficiencies are explicitly configurable heuristics, not reverse-engineered proprietary constants. Estimates are not a guarantee of OOM avoidance or throughput. Batching and concurrency are distinct: user demand can exceed active batch capacity and cause queuing. No CPU/NVMe layer offloading is simulated in this build.

Model architectures are representative and editable; verify exact model configuration with its official config. 1 GiB = 2³⁰ bytes; bandwidth values are decimal GB/s.
